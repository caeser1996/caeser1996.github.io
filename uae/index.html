Who this is for

The UAE is moving quickly on AI and digital services, and many organisations here need senior technical leadership faster than they can hire it. I work with:

  • Founders and scale-ups in Dubai and Abu Dhabi who need a CTO-level partner for architecture, hiring and investor conversations.
  • Enterprises and family groups modernising legacy systems and setting an AI strategy that will stand up to scrutiny.
  • Healthcare and regulated organisations that need to use sensitive data responsibly and keep it in-country.
  • Investors and funds that want an independent technical view before they commit capital.

The problems I solve

AI strategy that turns into delivery

Many AI programmes in the region start with ambition and a vendor shortlist. I help pick the use cases that pay back, ground them in your own data and get one into production with evaluation and cost controls. The detail is on my AI and LLM architecture page.

Data protection and residency

Companies may fall under the federal Personal Data Protection Law, or under the separate DIFC or ADGM data-protection regimes if they are based in those free zones, along with sector rules and customer expectations about where data lives. I design architecture that maps data flows, keeps regulated data in the right place and records who accessed what. I work alongside your legal advisers, who interpret the law; my job is to make the systems match their advice.

Private AI for sensitive data

When data cannot leave the country, or the building, I design private AI setups: in-country cloud regions, on-prem open-weight models and gateways that route by sensitivity. I wrote more about this in running private LLMs on-prem.

Leadership gaps

Senior technical hires take time. A fractional CTO gives you that judgement now and helps you hire the permanent leader.

My approach

  1. Understand the business and the regulatory context. Free zone or mainland, sector rules, customer commitments.

  4. Deliver a first production win. One use case, measured and governed.
  5. Build the team. Hiring, mentoring and an operating rhythm that works across time zones.

Why a regional view matters

Much of the advice written about AI and data assumes a US or European regulatory setting and US-hosted cloud services. In the UAE the picture is different in a few practical ways.

  • More than one data-protection regime. Mainland companies, DIFC entities and ADGM entities can face different rules, and groups often span several of them.
  • Residency expectations. Government, healthcare and financial customers frequently expect data to stay in the country, which shapes cloud region choice and AI vendor choice.
  • Arabic and English together. Search, retrieval and AI assistants often need to work across both languages, which affects model selection and evaluation.
  • Fast-moving national AI agenda. Expectations from boards and regulators are rising quickly, so governance has to be designed in, not added later.

I bring architecture patterns proven at scale, adapted to these constraints, and I am clear about where legal advice is needed and where engineering decisions can move ahead.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work on-site in the UAE?

I am based in the United States and the UAE and work with Gulf teams both remotely and in person when workshops or leadership sessions benefit from it.

How do you handle UAE data-protection requirements?

I design with the relevant regime in mind: the federal PDPL, or the DIFC and ADGM frameworks for companies in those free zones. That includes data mapping, residency, access control and audit trails. Legal interpretation stays with your counsel.

Can AI be used if data must stay in the UAE?

Yes. Options include in-country cloud regions and private, on-prem models for sensitive workloads, with only non-sensitive work sent to hosted APIs.

Can you work with teams split between the US, Europe and the Gulf?

Yes. I regularly work across US and Gulf time zones and set up operating rhythms that suit distributed teams.

What kinds of organisations do you work with in the region?

Startups and scale-ups, established enterprises modernising their technology, healthcare organisations, and investors who need technical due diligence.
