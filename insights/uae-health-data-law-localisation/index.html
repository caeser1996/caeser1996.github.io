The UAE health data law: designing health AI and cloud platforms for localisation

Health tech companies entering the UAE often plan around the general data protection law and miss the stricter rule that applies to health data. It shapes where your databases, models and support teams can be.

ExplainerSumanta Mukhopadhyay22 Oct 2026 · 12:15 GST7 min read4 sources
#healthcare#uae#regulation#ai

When health tech companies plan a UAE launch, the conversation usually starts with the federal Personal Data Protection Law, which I covered in UAE PDPL and AI architecture. For health data there is a more specific and stricter rule: the federal law on the use of information and communications technology in health fields, Federal Law No. 2 of 2019. Its localisation requirement is the one that most often changes an architecture.

As always, this is an architect's reading. Confirm the current position and any emirate-level rules with UAE counsel.

The core rule

Article 13 of the law provides that health data relating to health services provided in the UAE cannot be stored, processed, generated or transferred outside the country, unless permitted, such as by a resolution of a health authority in coordination with the Ministry of Health and Prevention14.

In 2021 the Ministry issued a resolution setting out exceptions. Commentary describes ten circumstances, including pharmacovigilance reporting, scientific research, insurance claims administration, and processing in the context of wearables and health monitoring devices23. Qualifying is not automatic: each exception carries conditions that organisations must be able to meet2.

Treat in-country as the default and every cross-border flow as an exception that needs a named legal basis and evidence that its conditions are met.

Where AI and cloud designs trip up

ComponentThe localisation question
Primary database and backupsAre both in a UAE region, including disaster recovery copies?
Model inferenceDoes any prompt containing health data reach a model hosted abroad?
Embeddings and vector storeAre embeddings of clinical text created and stored in the UAE?
Logs, traces and analyticsDo prompts, responses or identifiers land in a foreign observability tool?
Support and engineering accessCan staff abroad view production health data?
Wearables and devicesWhich exception applies, and are its conditions met?

The same hidden-transfer problem I described for Saudi PDPL and cross-border AI applies here, with less room for manoeuvre because the default is stricter.

The architecture I use

  1. A UAE data plane. Databases, object storage, backups, vector stores and analytics in UAE cloud regions or on-premises, with infrastructure policies that block resources elsewhere.
  2. In-country inference for anything touching health data: private models or in-region managed services, following the options in where to run models in the UAE.
  3. A gateway that enforces it, redacting or blocking health data before any call to services outside the country.
  4. Local observability, with a regional OpenTelemetry gateway as in Collector deployment patterns, and metadata-only export if anything must leave.
  5. Access controls by location for support and engineering, with break-glass procedures and logging.
  6. A register of exceptions, mapping any permitted transfer to the specific exception, its conditions and the evidence.

A regional platform

For companies serving several Gulf markets, I design one platform with a data plane per jurisdiction and a shared control plane that never touches patient data. It costs more than a single region, but it is far cheaper than re-platforming after a regulator or a hospital client asks where their data lives.

FAQ

Common questions

Can UAE health data be stored outside the country?

Generally not. Article 13 of the UAE's law on the use of ICT in health fields restricts storing, processing, generating or transferring health data outside the UAE unless permitted, for example under exceptions set by ministerial resolution.

What exceptions exist?

A 2021 Ministry of Health and Prevention resolution sets out a number of circumstances where transfers may be permitted, which commentary describes as including pharmacovigilance reporting, scientific research, insurance claims administration and wearables and health monitoring devices, each subject to conditions.

How does this affect health AI products?

Model hosting, vector stores, logs, analytics and support access all need to be designed so UAE health data stays in the country unless a specific exception and its conditions apply. In-country cloud regions and private models are common answers.
