Fractional CTO

Fractional CTO: senior technical leadership, without the full-time hire

I work as a part-time CTO for founders, CEOs and boards who need experienced technical judgement now, and a team that can carry it on after I step back.

Book a 30-min call → Analyse your challenge

Who this is for

Most companies that call me are not short of engineers. They are short of someone who can sit with the CEO in the morning, review a pull request in the afternoon and explain the trade-off to the board the next day. That is the job of a CTO, and plenty of businesses need it long before they can justify, or attract, a full-time one.

You are probably a good fit if any of these sound familiar:

  • You are a non-technical founder and every technical decision feels like a bet you cannot check.
  • Your engineering lead is excellent at shipping but has never set a multi-year technical strategy or reported to a board.
  • You have an AI initiative that impressed in a demo and has stalled since.
  • Investors are asking about scalability, security or AI, and the honest answers are vague.
  • Your last CTO left and you need steady leadership while you find the right replacement.

If you are still deciding whether you need this at all, I wrote a longer piece on when you actually need a fractional CTO.

The problems I solve

Decisions that are expensive to reverse

Choice of cloud, data model, build versus buy, which AI vendor to commit to. These decisions set your cost base and your speed for years. I make sure they are made deliberately, written down and tied to business outcomes rather than whatever was trendy that quarter.

A team that has outgrown its structure

Ten engineers can coordinate over coffee. Forty cannot. I design team topology, ownership boundaries and the engineering practices (reviews, decision records, on-call) that let more people ship without stepping on each other.

AI ambition without a plan

I help you pick the AI use cases that pay for themselves, ground them in your own data, and put evaluation and cost controls in place before you scale. My AI architecture work covers the detail.

Technology the board cannot see into

I translate engineering reality into the language of risk, cost and timeline, so your board and investors get honest, useful reporting instead of reassurance.

How I approach a fractional CTO engagement

  1. Listen first. In the first two weeks I talk to founders, engineers, product and key customers, and read the code and the cloud bill. Opinions come later.
  2. Write the current state down. A short, candid assessment of architecture, team, delivery and risk. Most teams have never seen their situation on one page.
  3. Agree a technical strategy. A roadmap tied to business milestones, with the few decisions that matter most called out and owned.
  4. Set up the operating rhythm. Architecture decision records, planning, metrics and a light governance process the team actually uses.
  5. Hire and grow people. I help define roles, interview senior candidates and mentor the engineers who will lead after me.
  6. Hand over cleanly. The goal is a team that runs without me, or a permanent CTO who inherits a clear picture rather than a mess.

How an engagement runs

We start with a free 30-minute call to see whether there is a fit. If there is, most clients begin with a two to four week architecture and AI readiness review. It produces a written assessment and a prioritised roadmap, and it is useful whether or not we continue.

From there, the fractional CTO role usually runs at one to two days a week, with a regular cadence: a weekly session with the leadership team, time embedded with engineering, and a monthly written update. The commitment flexes up around fundraises, launches and senior hires, and down once the team is running well.

Fractional, interim or full-time?

OptionBest whenWatch out for
Fractional CTOYou need senior judgement now, but not five days a weekNeeds a strong engineering lead day to day
Interim CTOA full-time seat is empty and must be covered for a defined periodHigher cost; focus can drift to firefighting
Full-time CTOTechnology is the business and the scale justifies itSlow and expensive to hire, costly to get wrong

Many of my clients use a fractional engagement to bridge the gap: getting the strategy and team right first, then hiring a permanent CTO who walks into a clear plan.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is an experienced technology leader who works with you part-time. You get CTO-level strategy, architecture and hiring decisions for a fraction of the cost of a full-time executive.

How many days a week does a fractional CTO work?

It depends on the stage. Most of my engagements start at one to two days a week, with more time during critical periods such as a fundraise, a platform rebuild or a key hire.

What is the difference between a fractional CTO and an interim CTO?

An interim CTO usually fills a vacant full-time seat for a fixed period. A fractional CTO works part-time over a longer stretch and is often the right fit before you can justify, or find, a full-time CTO.

Can a fractional CTO help us hire a full-time CTO?

Yes. Hiring and handing over to a permanent technical leader is one of the most common ways my engagements end. I help write the role, assess candidates and run a clean transition.

Do you only work with startups?

No. I work with startups, scale-ups and established organisations that need senior technical leadership for a specific initiative, such as an AI programme or a platform modernisation.
