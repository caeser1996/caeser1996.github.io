What shapes technology decisions in Saudi Arabia

The Personal Data Protection Law has been enforceable since September 2024, administered by the Saudi Data and AI Authority13. Transfers of personal data outside the Kingdom need an appropriate basis and safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, with risk assessments required in some cases2. For AI systems that often call hosted models abroad, the design question is what data leaves the country, and whether it needs to.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with companies in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Riyadh is one hour behind the UAE, so I work on Saudi hours and travel for workshops when they add value.

How does the PDPL affect sending data to AI providers abroad?

Transfers outside the Kingdom need an appropriate basis and safeguards, and risk assessments apply in some cases. I design architectures that keep sensitive processing in the country and minimise what leaves it; your counsel confirms the legal position.

Can you build AI that works well in Arabic?

Yes. I design bilingual retrieval, evaluation sets that score Arabic and English separately, and model selection based on your own questions.
