What shapes technology decisions in Australia

The OAIC has published guidance on privacy for organisations using commercially available AI products and for those developing and training generative AI models, including due diligence, accuracy and care with personal information12. For APRA-regulated entities, CPS 230 on operational risk management has applied since 1 July 2025, covering critical operations, business continuity and material service providers34.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with Australian companies?

Yes, remotely. Sydney afternoons overlap Gulf mornings, and I schedule regular sessions in that window, with written updates in between.

What does CPS 230 mean for our technology?

CPS 230 has applied to APRA-regulated entities since 1 July 2025, covering operational risk, business continuity and material service providers. I design critical operations, tolerance levels and vendor exit plans into the architecture.

Can staff use generative AI tools with customer data?

The OAIC recommends not entering personal information, especially sensitive information, into publicly available generative AI tools. I help set up private or enterprise options with the right controls.
