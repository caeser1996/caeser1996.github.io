Fractional CTO and AI architect for US companies

Senior technology leadership for US founders, enterprises and investors: AI that reaches production, data platforms that pass security review, and governance that keeps pace with federal and state rules.

Who this is for

  • Seed to Series B startups that need CTO-grade judgement on architecture, hiring and investor diligence before a full-time CTO makes sense.
  • Scale-ups whose platform is straining under growth, or whose AI pilots are stuck before production.
  • Healthcare and life sciences companies building on clinical data, where interoperability and privacy decide what is possible.
  • Investors who want an independent technical view before they commit.

What shapes technology decisions in the US

There is no single federal AI law. Instead, buyers expect a recognised security attestation, sector rules apply to the data you hold, and a growing number of states are legislating on AI. Health data falls under HIPAA1; enterprise customers usually ask for a SOC 2 report built on the AICPA Trust Services Criteria3; and many organisations use the voluntary NIST AI Risk Management Framework to structure AI governance2. States such as Colorado have passed laws aimed at high-risk AI decisions4, so it pays to design for documentation and impact assessment early.

Typical first projects

  1. Getting an AI or RAG pilot into production with evaluations, tracing and a known cost per query.
  2. Re-architecting a platform for scale: event-driven cores that have carried 1M+ transactions a day.
  3. A HIPAA-aligned clinical data platform on FHIR and OMOP.
  4. Technical due diligence ahead of a funding round or acquisition.

Time zones

I work on US hours for US clients, with Eastern and Pacific time both covered. Teams split between the US and Europe, the Middle East or India get an operating rhythm with written decisions, so nobody waits a day for an answer.

How engagements work

  • Architecture and AI readiness review (2 to 4 weeks): a clear picture of the current platform, risks and a prioritised roadmap.
  • Fractional CTO (ongoing, part-time): technical strategy, architecture governance, hiring and board-level communication.
  • Build and hand-off: hands-on delivery of a platform or AI capability, documented so your team can run it.
  • Technical due diligence: an independent view of a target's technology for investors and acquirers.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with US companies directly?

Yes. I am based in India, the United States and the UAE and work with US teams on US hours, remotely and on site for workshops when it adds value.

Can you help us pass a SOC 2 audit with AI features?

I design the controls into the architecture: access, logging, change management and vendor management for model providers. The audit itself is done by an independent CPA firm.

Do you work with healthcare companies under HIPAA?

Yes. I design HIPAA-aligned platforms with FHIR and OMOP, de-identification, audit trails and least-privilege access. Legal interpretation stays with your counsel.
