What shapes technology decisions in Singapore

The PDPC's advisory guidelines, issued in March 2024, explain how the PDPA's consent and notification obligations, and their exceptions, apply when personal data is used to develop and deploy AI recommendation and decision systems1. Financial institutions are expected to observe the MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines on governance, resilience and third-party oversight2. Singapore's AI Verify Foundation provides open testing tools for responsible AI3.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with Singapore companies?

Yes, remotely, with Singapore mornings overlapping my Gulf working day. I travel for workshops when they add value.

Can we use customer personal data to build AI under the PDPA?

Often yes, with care. The PDPC's advisory guidelines explain when consent is needed and when exceptions such as business improvement or research may apply. I design data flows so the basis for each use is clear.

Do you work with MAS-regulated firms?

Yes. I design to the expectations in the MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines: governance, resilience, third-party oversight and security.
