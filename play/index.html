Orbital Ops: defend production from incoming incidents

A free sci-fi arcade game that runs in your browser. Shoot down latency spikes, memory leaks and prompt injections, survive the legacy monolith, and protect your error budget. Switch on the ambient soundtrack with the sound button.

Score0Wave0Streak×1 Error budget Best0

How to play

Move your ship with the mouse, a finger, the arrow keys or WASD. Firing is automatic, so you can focus on dodging. Every eight incidents you clear without letting one escape raises your streak multiplier, up to eight times. Each hit spends one point of your error budget; lose all three and the game ends. Press P to pause at any time.

Power-ups

  • Autoscale: triple shot for a few seconds.
  • Observability: slows incoming incidents so you can see what is happening.
  • Circuit breaker: a shield that absorbs one hit.
  • Rollback: restores one point of error budget.

Incident field guide

FAQ

Common questions

What is Orbital Ops?

A free browser arcade game on this site. You pilot a ship defending a production system from incidents such as latency spikes, memory leaks, schema drift, traffic floods and prompt injections, with a legacy monolith boss every fifth wave.

How do I play?

Move with the mouse, a finger, the arrow keys or WASD. Firing is automatic. Collect power-ups, keep your streak for a higher multiplier and protect your error budget of three hits. Press P to pause.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, with touch controls. Nothing is installed and no account is needed; your best score is kept only in your own browser.

Is the music real?

The ambient soundtrack is generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API, so there is nothing to download. It stays off until you switch it on with the sound button.
