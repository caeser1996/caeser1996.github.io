What shapes technology decisions in India

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is now being brought into force: the DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025, with obligations phasing in over 18 months123. Consent-manager registration follows after a year, and most core duties for data fiduciaries apply from mid-20273. The engineering implication is consent and purpose tracking at the data level, retention that actually deletes, and breach detection you can evidence.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with Indian startups and GCCs?

Yes. India is one of my bases, alongside the United States and the UAE, so I work on Indian hours and meet teams in person when it helps. I also help global companies set up and lead engineering teams in India.

When do the DPDP Rules apply?

The Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and phase in over 18 months, with most core obligations for data fiduciaries applying from May 2027. I design consent, notice, retention and breach handling into the platform ahead of that.

Can you help us scale a high-volume platform?

Yes. I have designed and scaled event-driven systems carrying more than a million transactions a day, with idempotent processing and clear SLOs.
