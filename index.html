Case study · Banking · Saudi Arabia

Raising the cost of a fake face: deepfake-resistant customer onboarding for a Saudi digital bank

A Saudi digital bank onboarded customers entirely through its app, using a selfie video matched to an ID document. Deepfake tools were making that check cheaper to beat. The goal was to raise the cost of a fake identity well above the cost of the defences, without making genuine customers suffer.

Case study · Recruitment technology · South Korea

High-impact by design: running AI candidate screening under South Korea's AI Basic Act

A South Korean recruitment platform used AI to match candidates to roles and summarise applications for employers. Under the AI Basic Act, AI used in hiring sits in the high-impact category. The goal was to keep the efficiency while making every AI-assisted step transparent, overseen by people and evidenced.

Case study · Managed IT services · United Kingdom

Ready before it is law: preparing a UK managed service provider for the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill

A mid-sized UK managed service provider held administrator access to hundreds of customer environments. The Cyber Security and Resilience Bill would bring providers like it under the NIS regulations for the first time. The goal was to get ahead of it: control privileged access centrally, detect and report incidents quickly, and tell affected customers promptly.

Case study · Manufacturing · European Union

Access by design: building an EU Data Act-ready data platform for a maker of connected equipment

A European manufacturer of connected heating and ventilation equipment collected rich product data, but only its own service teams could use it. The EU Data Act turned that into an obligation: users must be able to access the data their products generate and share it with others. The goal was to build that into the product platform rather than bolt on a support process.
