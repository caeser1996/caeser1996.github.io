An AI copilot for the security operations centre that analysts trust

A security operations team faced more alerts than analysts, from tools that each spoke their own data format. Leadership wanted AI to help. Analysts wanted it not to invent things or take actions on its own. The goal was a copilot that speeds up triage and investigation while every response stays a human decision.

Case study · Utilities · UAE

One knowledge base, two languages: a bilingual Arabic and English assistant for UAE customers

A UAE utility's customers asked the same questions in Gulf Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, English and a mix of all three. Its knowledge lived in policies written in one language or the other. The goal was an assistant that answers in the customer's language, from the right source, with a citation, and keeps personal data in the country.

Case study · Fintech · UAE

Building a fintech platform for UAE Open Finance: consent, security and a single hub

A UAE fintech had built its product on screen scraping and one-off bank integrations. The Central Bank's Open Finance framework offered a regulated, standardised alternative through a central API hub. The goal was to re-platform around consent and secure APIs without stalling the product roadmap.
