Case study · Insurance · Australia

When the model is a material service provider: AI resilience for an Australian insurer under CPS 230

An Australian general insurer had put AI into claims intake, customer service and fraud review, mostly through one hosted model provider. Nobody had asked what happens when that provider is slow, out of capacity or gone. Under APRA's CPS 230 that is a board-level question, and the goal was a credible answer.

Case study · Travel · Singapore & Southeast Asia

When the customer is an agent: making a Southeast Asian travel marketplace ready for AI assistants

A regional travel marketplace saw consumer AI agents arrive in its home market, able to compare, basket and book on a person's behalf. Its site was built for humans and scrapers. The goal was to give agents a fast, safe and honest way in, without breaking the experience for everyone else.

Case study · Consumer lending · India

Consent that actually propagates: preparing an Indian digital lender for the DPDP Rules

An Indian digital lender collected consent in its app but could not say, for any one customer, which partners held their data, for what purpose, or whether a withdrawal had reached them. With the DPDP Rules phasing in, the goal was a consent and data-rights platform that turns legal obligations into events every system honours.

Case study · Insurance · United States

From emergency patch to modern platform: securing a legacy PHP estate without a big-bang rewrite

A US specialty insurer ran its agent portal, quoting tools and dozens of marketing sites on PHP, some of it years out of support. When an actively exploited PHP flaw landed, nobody could say quickly where PHP was running. The goal was to close that exposure in days, then turn a one-off scramble into a platform that stays patched.
