Case study · Retail · Japan

Ready before it bites: AI data governance for a Japanese retail chain under the amended APPI

A Japanese retail chain used loyalty data to forecast demand and store cameras to understand footfall, with several contractors doing the analytics. Japan's amended privacy law is now on the books, adding surcharges, rules for facial data and a route for using personal data in statistics and AI development. The duties are not in force yet. The aim was to make the data estate ready before they are.

Case study · Payments · Nigeria

Onshore by January: moving Nigerian payment data home without breaking a pan-African platform

A Lagos-based payments company ran one platform for Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana from an overseas cloud region. The Central Bank of Nigeria then set a deadline for payment transaction data generated in Nigeria to be stored in Nigeria. The task was to move that data onshore, keep one platform for every market and cut over without losing a payment.

Case study · Banking · Colombia

From voluntary to mandatory: an open finance platform for a mid-sized Colombian bank

Colombia has made open finance mandatory for supervised financial institutions. A mid-sized bank had to become both a reliable data provider and a capable data receiver, on standards still being written, without building twice. The answer was a platform designed around consent, security profiles and measurable service levels.

Case study · Utilities · United States

Guarding the keys: hardening the management plane of a US regional water and power utility

A regional utility had invested in protecting its control systems, but the tools that manage everything else, from firewalls and VPNs to endpoint and IT service platforms, sat on the ordinary corporate network. That is exactly where state-backed attackers have been looking. The goal was to make those management tools hard to reach, hard to abuse and impossible to use silently.
