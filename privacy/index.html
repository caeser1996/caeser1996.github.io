Privacy and cookies

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts and no forms, and it never asks for or stores your name or email. Last updated 28 October 2026.

What is collected

With your consent, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how it is used: pages viewed, how far you scroll, which links and buttons you use (for example booking a call, opening an article or answering the challenge assessor), approximate location at country or city level, device and browser type, and how you arrived. This data is anonymous to me: I cannot see who you are.

No personal information such as names, email addresses or phone numbers is sent to Google Analytics. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off.

Analytics cookies are only set after you accept them. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics is off until you choose to allow it. Elsewhere it is on by default and you can switch it off at any time.

Your choice

You can change your choice whenever you like: . Rejecting analytics has no effect on how the site works.

Third parties

Booking a call takes you to Google Calendar's appointment page, and writing to me opens your own email client or Gmail. Those services have their own privacy policies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and scripts from public CDNs, which may see your IP address when they deliver files.
