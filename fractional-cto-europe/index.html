What shapes technology decisions in the EU

The EU AI Act sets harmonised, risk-based rules for AI systems, with obligations phasing in over several years12. GDPR still governs any personal data the system touches4. In financial services, DORA has applied since 17 January 2025 and reaches the ICT third parties, including cloud and AI providers, that firms depend on3. Good architecture turns these into concrete artefacts: an AI inventory, risk classification, logging, human oversight and tested exit plans.

Typical first projects

  1. EU AI Act technical readiness: inventory, classification and evidence.
  2. Production AI with evaluations, tracing and sovereign or private model options.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you help us prepare for the EU AI Act?

Yes, on the technical side: inventorying AI systems, classifying risk, and building the documentation, logging, human oversight and evaluation evidence the Act expects. Legal classification stays with your counsel.

What does DORA mean for our cloud and AI vendors?

DORA applies to EU financial entities from 17 January 2025 and brings ICT third-party risk, including cloud and AI providers, into scope. I design exit plans, monitoring and incident reporting into the architecture.

Do you work in European time zones?

Yes. Central European hours overlap fully with my Gulf working day.
