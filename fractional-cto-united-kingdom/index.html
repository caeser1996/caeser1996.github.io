What shapes technology decisions in the UK

UK GDPR remains the foundation, now amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, which received Royal Assent in June 2025 and is being brought into force in stages12. The ICO's guidance on AI and data protection sets out how it interprets the law for AI systems, including fairness, transparency, data minimisation and individual rights34. For architecture, that means knowing exactly what personal data each model sees, why, and how a decision can be explained or contested.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with UK companies?

Yes, remotely on UK hours, with on-site workshops when they add value. Most of the working day overlaps with my Gulf hours.

Does the Data (Use and Access) Act change how we build AI?

It amends parts of UK data protection law, including rules on automated decision-making, and is being commenced in stages. I design systems so the safeguards are in place; your counsel confirms how the changes apply to you.

Do we need a data protection impact assessment for AI?

The ICO's view is that a DPIA is likely to be required before using AI to process personal data. I build the technical evidence a DPIA needs.
